## A Note on Names

PLACEHOLDER. Characters appear under their given names in pinyin. Where the source makes
a point of a courtesy name, it is glossed once and then dropped.

## The Source

The Chinese text is *Sanguo Yanyi* (三國演義), attributed to Luo Guanzhong, written in the
fourteenth century and long in the public domain. Chapters 1 to 20 are included in this
fixture.
